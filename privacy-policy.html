Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 18, 2026

Akanksha Digitals Solutions ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Puzzle Hub mobile game application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

By downloading, installing, or using Puzzle Hub, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

Puzzle Hub does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal information from users. We do not require account registration, login credentials, email addresses, phone numbers, names, or any other personally identifiable information.

Non-Personal Information

Puzzle Hub does not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, usage analytics, or other non-personal information for tracking or profiling purposes.

Device Permissions

The App may request access to device storage solely for the purpose of saving and retrieving game-related data, such as game progress, settings, or locally stored content. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.

2. How We Use Information

Since Puzzle Hub does not collect personal or non-personal user information, we do not use, process, analyze, sell, rent, or share user data.

Storage access is used exclusively to enable the App's functionality, including saving and loading game progress and settings on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

Puzzle Hub does not integrate with or use third-party services that collect user information, including but not limited to:

  • Analytics providers
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integrations
  • User authentication services
  • Cloud storage providers

If third-party services are added in future versions of the App, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Advertising

Puzzle Hub does not display advertisements and does not use advertising networks such as AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or similar services.

Therefore, no advertising-related data is collected, shared, or processed.

5. Analytics

Puzzle Hub does not use analytics services, including but not limited to Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or any similar analytics platform.

No user activity, device information, or usage statistics are collected through analytics tools.

6. Data Retention

Because we do not collect or store user information on our servers, we do not retain personal data.

Any game-related information saved by the App is stored locally on your device and remains under your control. You may delete this information at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage through your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Puzzle Hub is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect, store, or process personal information from children under 13 years of age. Since the App does not collect personal information from any users, no child-specific personal information is collected.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us by a child, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

8. Security

Although Puzzle Hub does not collect or store user information on our servers, we are committed to maintaining reasonable security practices to protect the integrity and functionality of the App.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their own devices and any data stored locally on those devices.

9. User Rights (GDPR and CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have certain privacy rights under applicable laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

These rights may include:

  • The right to know what personal information is collected.
  • The right to access personal information.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate information.
  • The right to request deletion of personal information.
  • The right to object to certain processing activities.
  • The right to data portability.
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights.

As Puzzle Hub does not collect or process personal information, most of these rights are not applicable in practice. However, users may contact us regarding any privacy-related concerns.

10. International Data Transfers

Puzzle Hub does not collect or transfer personal information across international borders.

Because no personal information is processed or stored on our servers, no international data transfers occur through the App.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements, App functionality, or business practices.

Any updates will be posted within the App, on our website, or through the Google Play Store listing. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will indicate when changes become effective.

Continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the App, please contact us:

Akanksha Digitals Solutions
Website: https://akankshadigitalsolutions.in/

13. Google Play Data Safety Disclosure

Based on the current functionality of Puzzle Hub:

  • No personal data is collected.
  • No user data is shared with third parties.
  • No analytics services are used.
  • No advertising services are used.
  • No account creation or login is required.
  • Storage permission is used only for local game functionality.
  • Data remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to the developer.

This Privacy Policy is intended to support Google Play Store compliance and should be reviewed and updated whenever the App's features or data practices change.
